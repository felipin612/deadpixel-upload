Every Dead Pixel game was built on the same lake.

The LAMPLIGHT disc has one map file. MERROW dot MAP. I opened it in a level viewer and turned the camera around.

The fog town from SALTMARSH is on the north shore. The ice fishing hut from STILLWATER is out on the frozen water. The signal box from SIGNALMAN sits by the old rail bridge. And at the end of the pier there's the lighthouse, with its beam fixed on one building. A boathouse. Fourteen Merrow Road.

Four games. One lake. And the light was never meant for the boats.
