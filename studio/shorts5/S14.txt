This PS2 lighthouse log has an entry from tomorrow.

Tomorrow's date, in pencil. One boat, no hull, coming in. And it's written in my handwriting.

The game is LAMPLIGHT, an unreleased Dead Pixel Studios game from 2003. You keep the light at the end of a pier, and you write every boat in the keeper's log. Every sweep, you count. If there's one more than you counted, you put the light out.

The log has forty pages. The last keeper filled in thirty nine. His final line says: eight boats, and one for Rowe. Then it stops. The third of March, 2004.
