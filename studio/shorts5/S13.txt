Every night this PS2 game shows me one extra boat.

Nine lamps on the lake. My counter says eight. The ninth has no hull. Just a lamp, sitting low on the black water.

The game is called LAMPLIGHT. Unreleased. Dead Pixel Studios, 2003. You keep a lighthouse at the end of a pier on Merrow Lake. One rule: count the boats on every sweep. If there's one more than you counted, put the light out.

I didn't put it out. The next night, the extra lamp was closer. Last night it was tied up at my pier, and the counter said zero.

The full story is on YouTube. Dead Pixel Studios.
