A PS2 train stopped outside my real house.

My blue door. My porch light, the one that flickers twice. Six dark carriages and one lit window.

The game is called SIGNALMAN. Unreleased. Dead Pixel Studios, 2002. You run a signal box. One rule: clear only the trains on the timetable.

The three oh three isn't on the timetable. And there are no train tracks within forty miles of my street.

The full story is on YouTube. Dead Pixel Studios.
