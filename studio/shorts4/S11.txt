Never clear a train that isn't on the timetable.

That's the only rule in SIGNALMAN, an unreleased PS2 railway game from Dead Pixel Studios.

On the second night, at three minutes past three, a light came on in the diagram. On a grey branch that runs off the edge of the map. Then the bell rang. Three beats, a pause, three beats. That code isn't on the list.

So I held it at red. It waited all night, ringing every few minutes. In the morning, the train register printed one new line. Held at signal. Driver: J. Kell.
