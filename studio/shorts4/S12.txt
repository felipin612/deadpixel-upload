Dead Pixel Studios finally has a founder's name.

A gaming magazine from the summer of 2002 previewed their railway game, SIGNALMAN. Half a page near the back. One quote from the founder, about growing up next to a branch line that closed. The name under the quote: J. Kell.

Then an old forum reply I had always skipped. Kell's dad drowned in Merrow Lake in sixty-one. She started the studio in his boathouse.

R. Kell. The man who went through the ice in 1961, one boot missing. His daughter made the games.

The full story is on YouTube. Dead Pixel Studios.
