Never use the rear camera in this PS2 game.

NIGHTWALK only lets you look forward. But the debug menu has one option nobody was supposed to touch. Rear camera.

I pressed it. For the first time I saw my character. A grey figure with a lantern. And ten steps behind him, another one. Same hood. No lantern.

I switched back and walked. Switched again. Five steps. Then two.

He wasn't walking when I walked. He was walking when I looked.
