There is a number in the corner of the screen. Steps behind you: one. I have not moved. I am sitting on my bed, and the number just went up to two.

The game is called NIGHTWALK. An unreleased PS2 game from a studio that never shipped anything. One road. One lantern. One rule. Do not look back.

Every night the counter goes up. And when I stop walking, the footsteps don't stop right away.

Last night it said fourteen. And under it, a line I had never seen. Distance: one step.
