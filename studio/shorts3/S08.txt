Never reel in when the fishing line goes still.

That's the only rule in STILLWATER, an unreleased PS2 ice fishing game. If the line goes still, cut it.

On the third night, my float stopped in the middle of a ripple. I reeled anyway. It came up through the hole. A blue dog collar. Biscuit. My dog, when I was seven. I left the gate open.

After that, the hole in the ice got bigger.
