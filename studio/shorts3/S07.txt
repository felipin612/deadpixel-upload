This PS2 fishing game caught my dead father's watch.

His real watch. Cracked strap, the scratch from the boat ramp. He lost it in 1998. He drowned in 1999. I never told anyone.

The game is called STILLWATER. Unreleased. Dead Pixel Studios, 2003. Every catch goes into a log, with the name of whoever lost it.

And at the bottom of the log, there is already one entry I haven't caught yet.

The full story is on YouTube. Dead Pixel Studios.
